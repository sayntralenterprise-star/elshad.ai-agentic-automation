# S1.8 Demo Script — Observability v1

1. Open the Console and navigate to **Observability**.
2. Review the **Overview & Dashboards** tab: verify total runs, success rate, P50/P95/P99 latency, and cost per successful outcome.
3. Switch to **Cost & Usage** tab: inspect per-agent and per-model token/cost breakdown and export CSV.
4. Switch to **Alerts & Incidents** tab:
   - Create an alert rule (e.g., `Failure Rate > 0.1` over a 5-minute window).
   - Trigger a synthetic failed run in Playground or via API.
   - Click **Evaluate Rules** (or wait for scheduled evaluation) and verify a `firing` incident appears.
   - Acknowledge and resolve the incident.
5. Inspect a failed run with **Failure Explainer** to view the classified root cause, failing step, and remediation guidance, and click **Export OTel Trace** to download OTLP GenAI JSON.
