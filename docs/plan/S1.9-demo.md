# S1.9 Demo Script — Pilot Agents

1. Navigate to **Pilot Agents** in the Console.
2. Inspect **Support Triage & Reply Drafter (L1)** and **Invoice Exception Handler (L2)** specifications, limits, and S.4 Go-Live Checklists.
3. Click **Run Golden Benchmarks** to execute both golden sets and verify all KPI targets pass (green).
4. Test **Support Triage (L1)** with a sample ticket (verify PII redaction, citations, and escalation on churn/legal keywords).
5. Test **Invoice Exception (L2)** with a sample tolerance mismatch (`<$50` & `<1%` auto-resolve) and a high-value mismatch (`>$10,000` requiring dual approval).
6. Click **Record Stakeholder Sign-Off** to persist go-live sign-off to the audit log.
